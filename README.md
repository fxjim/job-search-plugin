# Job Search by Pixel & Cog

**0.3.3-pilot.3 — public preparation-only prerelease, MIT licensed.** Install the workflow in a supported local host, then let your agent guide setup using your own goals and experience. It researches accessible openings, checks evidence and gaps, drafts truthful materials and tracks duplicates. It stops at your reviewed packet.

No web app, dashboard, product account, paid API, MCP server, telemetry or hosted backend is included. The pilot does not submit applications, fill employer forms, upload resumes, send email, spend money or contact employers. Your host and connected research services have their own permissions and data policies.

## Get started

1. Read [installation](INSTALL.md) and choose the Git-backed marketplace or dedicated-project skill route.
2. Obtain the [local source + installer ZIP](https://github.com/fxjim/job-search-plugin/releases/download/v0.3.3-pilot.3/job-search-0.3.3-pilot.3-local.zip) and [checksums](https://github.com/fxjim/job-search-plugin/releases/download/v0.3.3-pilot.3/SHA256SUMS), or use the pinned source tag. 3. Ask your agent: “Walk me through my job-search setup for research and drafts only.” Confirm your profile and storage before saving; review every exact material before manual use.

## Sources and honest limits

Preloaded categories include employer career pages; employer-scoped Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, Breezy, Jobvite, Rippling and Gem; YC and a16z portfolio jobs; Wellfound and CryptoCurrencyJobs; opt-in connected Gmail job alerts, including Indeed email alerts only; employer-published application email for manual handoff; optional read-only Upwork through a supported connector. No global ATS feed, exhaustive coverage, automatic connection or scrape permission is promised. Prop-firm providers and dedicated boards are excluded; ordinary finance employers remain allowed.

Source-method checks retain their actual October 3, 2026 timestamps. Current postings, eligibility and pay need verification. Base salary, OTE and contract rates stay distinct; unknown facts remain unknown. AI-assisted work is attributed accurately.

Package/schema, archive integrity and isolated project installation are checked. All 18 skill files are unchanged from the preceding MIT candidate. Earlier synthetic workflow evidence is version-qualified. **Independent recipient host loading and genuine setup acceptance are still untested for this release.** macOS is the tested local installer target; Linux/Windows and web/mobile-only installation are not accepted. No live application, delivery or hiring outcome is claimed. See [release status](plugins/job-search/RELEASE-STATUS.md) and [recipient checklist](plugins/job-search/ACCEPTANCE-CHECKLIST.md).

This GitHub prerelease is separate from the official OpenAI directory. No official-directory submission or approval is claimed.
