# Use Job Search

Ask your agent to walk you through setup for research and drafts only. It asks a few related questions at a time about where/how you want to work, roles, industries, base or contract pay floor, availability, your resume/project evidence and the sources you can access. Missing answers remain unknown. Pasted resume/posting text is acceptable when tools or files are unavailable. You do not need an API key or a new product account.

The agent shows the setup and proposed private storage location before saving. You can correct it. Your own exclusions stay distinct from package rules: this release excludes prop-firm providers and boards, but does not invent that as your preference. No other customer's profile or permissions are included.

It then checks a small set of sources, labels freshness/access gaps, evaluates fit against cited evidence and prepares truthful resume text, a cover note and proposed answers. Base pay is separate from OTE, bonuses and contract values. Self-report is labeled; unseen project files, dates, credentials and metrics are never invented. AI assistance is attributed accurately. Review all exact content before recording approval. A reviewed packet remains prepared, not submitted.

This pilot does not enter your information into forms, upload a resume, send email, apply, spend or contact employers. It gives you a verified employer route and manual handoff. Current posting/form and required answers remain your final check. Prior outcomes and uncertain applications matter for duplicates; do not retry an ambiguous action elsewhere without checking receipt evidence.

After plugin installation, type `@job` and select Job Search, or choose Try Now in its installed detail page. Project-only skill installation relies on that host's actual project discovery. Missing discovery is an access/setup issue to report, not a reason to repeat installation indefinitely.
